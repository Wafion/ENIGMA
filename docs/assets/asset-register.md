### Caption rules

- Every figure prints creator, date and licence under the image.
- Every photograph is “modern contextual photography” unless it is a Library of Congress map. None is a production still.
- `seneca-creek-woods.jpg` is **2022**, not 1997. The prologue says “Contemporary location photograph, 2022”.
- `sony-hi8-camcorder.jpg` is a representative 1990s model, not the exact unit purchased for the shoot. The Chapter 05 copy says so.
- `patapsco-grist-mill-tunnel.jpg` shows park stonework, not the demolished Griggs House.
- The four cast/filmmaker portraits are contemporary photographs, not 1997 publicity material.

### ShareAlike and attribution

CC BY-SA images require attribution and share-alike on redistribution. The essay credits each image in the caption and in the asset dialog. If the images are cropped or colour-graded, the attribution and licence remain. Re-check every Wikimedia file page before commercial distribution.

### Audio honesty

- They are **not** Pixabay downloads. The old Stage 1 note said Pixabay was blocked and the beds were generated locally; that remains true.
- They are **not** field recordings of Seneca Creek, the Griggs House, or the 1997 shoot. The asset descriptions say so.
- The sound menu now says “Sound design made for this essay”, and audio objects say “Sound design · project original”.
- Audio never autoplays. It starts only after “Turn sound on”, pauses when the tab is hidden, and can be muted or turned off.
- The browser's native `<audio controls>` is used in the asset dialog. There is no decorative waveform.

## 5. Gaps — do not fake

- **No licensed stills of the 1999 website.** The BlairWitch.com section is a labelled reconstruction of editorial framing, not a screenshot. The original is at the Internet Archive.
- **No film frames.** The recording study is procedural SVG. Do not replace it with stills unless the rights are cleared.
- **No Griggs House interior photographs.** The house was demolished in December 1999. Do not invent an interior.
- **No 1997 production photographs.** The modern location photographs are not evidence of the shoot.
- **No AI-generated forest imagery as evidence.** None is used.
- **No third-party audio.** The beds are synthesised. Do not relabel them as field recordings.
